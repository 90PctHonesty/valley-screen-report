Dune: Part Three Not on sale

Dec 17–20, wide Dec 18

Correction, same day. An earlier version of this page said Dune had gone on sale at Harkins, and led with two IMAX 70mm Insider Screenings at Arizona Mills on Dec 14 and 15. That was wrong, and it was wrong in a way worth explaining because it will come up again.

The schedule data does contain Dune sessions — Dec 14 and 15 at Arizona Mills flagged IMAX and 70mm, and a general CINÉ XL run from Dec 17 across eleven Valley houses. Each one has a session ID, a ticketing URL, and soldOut: false. None of that means purchasable. A showing with no inventory loaded looks identical to a showing with every seat free, because the sold-out flag defaults to false either way.

The test that actually works Harkins’ own ticketing service will say so directly. Asked about a Dune session it replies success: false, reason: "Bad showtime session."; a past showing returns "Expired showtime."; a genuinely on-sale one returns a seat plan with exact counts. Checked today: Castle in the Sky on Oct 10 — eight weeks out — answers with 96 of 98 seats open. Both Dune sessions refuse, the Insider IMAX 70mm nights and the general Dec 18 run alike. Distance is not the reason. Dune is simply not on sale.
This page has made this mistake before On Aug 12 it reported that Dune IMAX 70mm seats had reopened at Arizona Mills, then reverted it the same day once the open seats turned out to be ADA and companion holds. Same film, same screen, same failure to distinguish a record existing from a ticket existing.
What is still true General on-sale has not happened. The Dec 14 and 15 Insider dates are real scheduled bookings and are the first sign of IMAX 70mm intent at Arizona Mills, which is worth watching — but they are not yet buyable. Watch the Harkins email list, then imax.com. The seat map is the confirmation; a session ID alone is not.
