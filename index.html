Dune: Part Three Not on sale

Dec 17–20 · wide Dec 18

The April presale is gone — opening-weekend IMAX 70mm at Arizona Mills sold out. Wave one went on sale April 6 with about half an hour's public notice, crashed the AMC app, and resold for around $1,000. Harkins added a second daily showtime days later; that went too. That was a presale at one theater, not the general on-sale, which still has not happened. What did change today is that imax.com's Dune page now carries date tabs for Dec 25, 26 and 27 alongside the original Dec 17–20. That is the run past opening weekend this page has been predicting. Be careful reading it: the theatre list underneath does not change when you switch dates, so the tabs are evidence of a run, not of purchasable seats.

Unconfirmed — a date is circulating A widely-shared post claims IMAX and IMAX 70mm tickets for Dune: Part Three go on sale Tuesday Aug 18 at 9:00 AM PT, which is 9:00 AM here. Treat this as unverified. It traces to a single fan account; IMAX's own news page carries nothing about it, Harkins has announced nothing, and the only official IMAX article on the subject is still the April 6 one about wave one — which is also what the "tickets on sale now" headlines in search results are pointing at. It costs nothing to be at a keyboard Tuesday morning. It would cost something to treat this as fact.
What is still true General on-sale has not happened. The film has a three-week global IMAX exclusivity window, and Arizona Mills is one of only nineteen IMAX 70mm venues worldwide on IMAX's own participating list — the only one in Arizona. Both previous Dune drops were announced on Harkins' Facebook and email list first, with roughly thirty minutes' notice. Get on the list; that is the whole strategy. Arizona Mills is currently proving it will schedule 1:30 AM IMAX 70mm shows when demand justifies it, which is a realistic shape for the Dune overflow.

1800 Congress Ave. Austin's only IMAX — 400 seats, dual laser, six storeys, 2D and 3D. Attached to the state history museum, with its own entrance and evening hours. Programmes documentaries and occasional event bookings rather than a normal release slate, which is why an IMAX-led title reaching Austin is not a given.

Out-of-market 70mm

Austin has no 70mm. The two nearest film projectors are AMC Rivercenter 11 in San Antonio (about 80 miles) and Cinemark Dallas on Webb Chapel (about 195 miles) — the only IMAX 70mm in Texas. AMC blocks automated checks, so Rivercenter showtimes have to be confirmed by hand.
