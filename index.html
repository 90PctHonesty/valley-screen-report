Dec 17–20, wide Dec 18

Correction, same day. An earlier version of this page said Dune had gone on sale at Harkins, and led with two IMAX 70mm Insider Screenings at Arizona Mills on Dec 14 and 15. That was wrong, and it was wrong in a way worth explaining because it will come up again.

The schedule data does contain Dune sessions — Dec 14 and 15 at Arizona Mills flagged IMAX and 70mm, and a general CINÉ XL run from Dec 17 across eleven Valley houses. Each one has a session ID, a ticketing URL, and soldOut: false. None of that means purchasable. The test that actually works Harkins’ own ticketing service will say so directly. drafthouse.com/austin

Alamo Drafthouse, five Austin locations — Village, South Lamar, Slaughter Lane, Lakeline and Mueller. The most complete single source in the city and it publishes months ahead. Series worth tracking by name: Terror Tuesday, Weird Wednesday, Video Vortex, Master Pancake, Movie Parties and Queer Film Theory 101.

austinfilm.org/screenings

AFS Cinema, the nonprofit arthouse founded by Richard Linklater. Posts a full programme about two months ahead with series labels attached — Big Screen Classics, Essential Cinema, World Cinema Classics, Newly Restored, Lates, Doc Nights and Fright Club. Currently fundraising to build a third screen.

austintheatre.org

The Paramount and State Theatres. The Summer Classic Film Series is the big annual event; the Paramount is closed for restoration, so the series is at the State and the Bullock IMAX. $15 regular, $11 Film Fan Club, $9 under-12, Flix-Tix ten-packs $90 or $75.

Bullock IMAX Theatre

1800 Congress Ave. Austin's only IMAX — 400 seats, dual laser, six storeys, 2D and 3D. Attached to the state history museum, with its own entrance and evening hours. Programmes documentaries and occasional event bookings rather than a normal release slate, which is why an IMAX-led title reaching Austin is not a given.

Out-of-market 70mm

Austin has no 70mm. The two nearest film projectors are AMC Rivercenter 11 in San Antonio (about 80 miles) and Cinemark Dallas on Webb Chapel (about 195 miles) — the only IMAX 70mm in Texas. AMC blocks automated checks, so Rivercenter showtimes have to be confirmed by hand.

Six IMAX screens exist in the Phoenix metro: Harkins Arizona Mills (the only one in Arizona with 70mm film capability, and one of 41 worldwide), AMC Mesa Grand, Desert Ridge, Deer Valley, Westgate, and Superstition East. Tucson's IMAX status is currently unverified — see the Tucson tab. Austin's only IMAX is the Bullock Texas State History Museum, which is dual laser rather than film.
