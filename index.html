Harkins is handing out collectible 35mm and 70mm film strips to 35mm and 70mm ticketholders while supplies last. Tucson has a 70mm run of the same film at The Loft, but there is no reason to drive for it — see the Tucson tab.

Dune: Part Three Not on sale

Dec 17–20 · wide Dec 18

The April presale is gone — opening-weekend IMAX 70mm at Arizona Mills sold out. Wave one went on sale April 6 with about half an hour's public notice, crashed the AMC app, and resold for around $1,000. Harkins added a second daily showtime days later; that went too. But that was a presale at one theater, not the general on-sale, which still has not happened. Nothing new surfaced today.

What's still coming General on-sale has not happened. The film has a three-week global IMAX exclusivity window, so a run past Dec 20 is close to certain, and venues have said more opening-weekend showtimes will be added. Both Dune drops were announced on Harkins' Facebook and email list first — get on the list, that is the whole strategy. Worth noting that Arizona Mills is currently proving it will schedule 1:30 AM IMAX 70mm shows when demand justifies it, which is a realistic shape for the Dune overflow.

Star Wars Not on sale

Fri Feb 19, 2027

The 1977 original, presented as audiences first saw it, for the 50th anniversary. The Prestige Format unconfirmed

Thu Jan 21, 2027

Nolan confirmed a 70mm upgrade for the 20th anniversary, describing 70mm blow-ups from the original 35mm anamorphic photography, with the conversion work starting in the weeks after the July announcement. Checking again today did not resolve the format question: several outlets report IMAX 70mm outright, while Nolan's own quoted remarks say only 70mm, and no distributor has clarified. That distinction decides whether Arizona Mills can play it in 1.43:1 or only as a standard 70mm print, so treat it as standard 70mm until Warner says otherwise.

Where this comes from

Check these yourself

cmiphx.com

Classic Movies In Phoenix. A hand-kept daily calendar of every repertory and anniversary screening in the metro, with ticket links per chain and honest "not on sale yet" flags. Better than anything the chains publish. Phoenix-only; there is no Tucson equivalent.

harkins.com page data

The site renders blank to automated reads, but the underlying page data carries per-showtime IMAX and 70mm flags and a theatre-level events catalogue. That is how both Odyssey 70mm runs and the LOTR series name were found. Presence in the catalogue is chain-wide, not proof of a local booking.

advancescreenings.com

Aggregates Gofobo plus the local outlets that actually get Phoenix allocations. Check the Tempe and Scottsdale pages too — most screenings land there, not in Phoenix proper.

redcarpetcrash.com

Their Phoenix category posts passes roughly a day before the aggregators. Worth checking direct. Mind on Movies' own front page still serves 2018 archive content, so use its links only through the aggregators.

Harkins email & Facebook

Where both Dune drops were announced first, with no press release and about thirty minutes' notice. The single highest-value thing on this list to subscribe to.

Pollack Tempe Cinemas

Currently polling on Facebook for future classic series. LOTR, the Mummy trilogy, Twilight and Stephen King are on the ballot. A vote is free.

Fri Sep 4, 2026

The IMAX 4K restoration. Nothing booked in Tucson, and there is now a real question about whether an IMAX-led release reaches the city at all. Correction — Tucson's IMAX status is not settled This page has been treating Galaxy Theatres Tucson as Tucson's confirmed IMAX screen. Today's check does not support that cleanly. Galaxy's Tucson Experiences page does list IMAX — but that page carries the identical chain-wide experiences text used at Galaxy's other locations, so it is not evidence about Tucson specifically. Meanwhile Tucson's actual showtime grid, read in full today, labels its premium auditoriums DFX (Barco 4K laser and Dolby Atmos) and Sony Digital Cinema, with no IMAX-labelled showtime anywhere in the schedule. The two sources conflict and neither is decisive. Until an IMAX showtime actually appears on Galaxy Tucson's grid, assume an IMAX-exclusive title may simply not play Tucson, and plan on Arizona Mills.
Where it shows up first galaxytheatres.com/movie-theater/tucson — the daily showtime grid, not the Experiences page. Any Tucson booking will surface there before it surfaces anywhere else.

The night after Phoenix's own Ghostbusters live-score show at Mesa Arts Center on Sep 30. Two different presentations of the same idea on consecutive nights a hundred miles apart — if the Mesa date sells out, this is the fallback, and the Fox is the better room.

Where this comes from

Check these yourself

loftcinema.org

The single most important Tucson source, carrying more of the load than any one Phoenix source does. Coming Soon gives dated listings about six weeks out with series labels. Confirm any specific date on the individual film page — the flattened showtimes grid is easy to misread.

The Loft, Screen 1

DCP, 35mm and 70mm — the only 70mm-capable screen in Tucson, currently running The Odyssey. This is the screen that makes The Prestige a live possibility here in January 2027.

foxtucson.com/events

Film is mixed into a music-and-comedy calendar under the Picture Palace filter, so scan the whole list rather than trusting the filter. Classics run $10 all in; live-score events run at concert prices.

galaxytheatres.com

Tucson's premium-format house. Trust the daily showtime grid, not the Experiences page and not the Flashback Cinema blog post, which is frequently months stale. The IMAX question above turns entirely on which of those you believe.

harkins.com/theatres/tucson-spectrum

CINÉ XL with Dolby Atmos, no IMAX. Chain-wide series are booked centrally, so a Valley series announcement usually means Tucson dates follow.

Tucson passes

Red Carpet Crash keeps a dedicated Tucson category and would be first to post one. advancescreenings.com has no registered Tucson outlets at all. The one named outlet, 43KIX Tucson, distributes through Facebook and cannot be read automatically. Expect this section to stay empty.

Six IMAX screens exist in the Phoenix metro: Harkins Arizona Mills (the only one in Arizona with 70mm film capability, and one of 41 worldwide), AMC Mesa Grand, Desert Ridge, Deer Valley, Westgate, and Superstition East. Tucson's IMAX status is currently unverified — see the Tucson tab.

Tucson is about a hundred miles from Tempe. An item earns its place on that tab by being 70mm, a film print, a live score, anime, or something that does not play the Valley at all.

Everything above was verified on the date shown. Sold-out status, showtimes and pass availability change fast — confirm before you drive.
