The one that got away

Dune: Part Three is sold out at Arizona Mills

All eight IMAX 70mm showings on opening weekend — two a day, Dec 17–20 — are gone. Arizona Mills is the only 1.43:1 IMAX screen in Arizona, and it was one of just 19 theaters worldwide in the April on-sale. General tickets have not been released yet. That is the next drop, and it will be announced on Harkins' own channels before anywhere else.

The watchlist

Not on sale yet — where the drop will surface

Akira Not on sale

Fri Sep 4, 2026

The 4K restoration in IMAX, from Crunchyroll and Sony, in both subtitled and dubbed versions. Announced July 2. No theater list, no on-sale date, and nothing on Harkins' site yet. Mid-August is the realistic window.

Where it shows up first Harkins files anime under a dedicated series page rather than a standalone listing, so harkins.com/events-and-series/anime-at-harkins will likely show it before any press release. Watch Crunchyroll's news page and @Crunchyroll too. Ignore the Akira IMAX pages already live on AMC and Fandango — those are leftovers from the 2020 run.

Dune: Part Three Sold out

Dec 17–20 · wide Dec 18

Opening-weekend IMAX 70mm at Arizona Mills is fully sold. Wave one went on sale April 6 with about half an hour's public notice, crashed the AMC app, and resold for around $1,000. Harkins added a second daily showtime days later; that went too.

What's still coming General on-sale has not happened. The film has a three-week global IMAX exclusivity window, so a run past Dec 20 is close to certain, and venues have said more opening-weekend showtimes will be added. Both Dune drops were announced on Harkins' Facebook and email list first — get on the list, that is the whole strategy.

LOTR Extended Editions Not on sale

Sep 4, 5, 6

All three extended cuts across three nights at Harkins. Roughly 3h48, 3h55 and 4h23 — about eleven and a half hours total. This is not the national Fathom event; that ran in January. It's a Harkins in-house booking, so there will be no national coverage announcing it.

What to expect When Harkins ran this before, it played in CINÉ XL — their large format with Dolby Atmos and 4K laser — at Arizona Mills, Tempe Marketplace, Chandler Fashion, SanTan Village, Scottsdale 101 and Estrella Falls, at $8 a film or $30 for a series pass including popcorn each night. If the series pass returns, it may sell before individual tickets.

Star Wars Not on sale

Fri Feb 19, 2027

The 1977 original, presented as audiences first saw it, for the 50th anniversary. IMAX confirmed the slot in its July investor presentation, but Lucasfilm hasn't announced ticket timing, theaters, run length, or whether any screenings use IMAX film projection. If you see April 30 listed anywhere, that's the old date.

The Prestige Format unconfirmed

Thu Jan 21, 2027

Nolan confirmed a 70mm upgrade for the 20th anniversary, describing 70mm blow-ups from the original 35mm anamorphic photography. Coverage has split on whether it's IMAX 70mm — Nolan himself didn't say IMAX. That distinction decides whether Arizona Mills can even play it, so treat it as standard 70mm until Warner says otherwise.
