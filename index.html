Fri Sep 4, 2026

The 4K restoration in IMAX, from Crunchyroll and Sony, in both subtitled and dubbed versions. Announced July 2. Watch Crunchyroll's news page and @Crunchyroll too. Neither reflects a purchasable Phoenix showtime — none exists yet.

Dune: Part Three Not on sale

Dec 17–20 · wide Dec 18

The April presale is gone — opening-weekend IMAX 70mm at Arizona Mills sold out. Wave one went on sale April 6 with about half an hour's public notice, crashed the AMC app, and resold for around $1,000. Harkins added a second daily showtime days later; that went too. But that was a presale at one theater, not the general on-sale, which still has not happened. Nothing new surfaced today.

What's still coming General on-sale has not happened. The film has a three-week global IMAX exclusivity window, so a run past Dec 20 is close to certain, and venues have said more opening-weekend showtimes will be added. Both Dune drops were announced on Harkins' Facebook and email list first — get on the list, that is the whole strategy.

LOTR Extended Editions Not on sale

Sep 4, 5, 6

All three extended cuts across three nights at Harkins. Roughly 3h48, 3h55 and 4h23 — about eleven and a half hours total. This is not the national Fathom event; that ran in January. It's a Harkins in-house booking, so there will be no national coverage announcing it. Harkins has movie pages up for all three nights, still with no tickets.

What to expect When Harkins ran this before, it played in CINÉ XL — their large format with Dolby Atmos and 4K laser — at Arizona Mills, Tempe Marketplace, Chandler Fashion, SanTan Village, Scottsdale 101 and Estrella Falls, at $8 a film or $30 for a series pass including popcorn each night. The pass is still not up: harkins.com/store/tickets-vouchers was rendered and read today and lists a $30 Harry Potter all-access pass and a $45 Studio Ghibli Fest pass, but nothing for LOTR. A product URL, harkins.com/store/products/lotr-series-pass, does exist in search indexes, but it renders empty and cannot be dated — it may simply be left over from the January run, so do not read it as a live listing. That the Harry Potter pass appeared roughly three weeks ahead of its run is still the best timing clue available. Two other chains are running the theatrical cuts around the same window — FatCats through August, with Two Towers now on sale for Aug 10–13, and RoadHouse Scottsdale on Sep 7–23.

Star Wars Not on sale

Fri Feb 19, 2027

The 1977 original, presented as audiences first saw it, for the 50th anniversary. IMAX confirmed the slot in its July investor presentation, but Lucasfilm still hasn't announced ticket timing, theaters, run length, or whether any screenings use IMAX film projection. If you see April 30 listed anywhere, that's the old date.

The Prestige Format unconfirmed

Thu Jan 21, 2027

Nolan confirmed a 70mm upgrade for the 20th anniversary, describing 70mm blow-ups from the original 35mm anamorphic photography. Coverage is still split and checking again today did not resolve it — several outlets report IMAX 70mm outright, while Nolan's own quoted remarks say only 70mm, and no distributor has clarified. That distinction decides whether Arizona Mills can even play it, so treat it as standard 70mm until Warner says otherwise.

Where this comes from

Check these yourself

cmiphx.com

Classic Movies In Phoenix. A hand-kept daily calendar of every repertory and anniversary screening in the metro, with ticket links per chain and honest "not on sale yet" flags. Better than anything the chains publish.

advancescreenings.com

Aggregates Gofobo plus the local outlets that actually get Phoenix allocations. Check the Tempe and Scottsdale pages too — most screenings land there, not in Phoenix proper.

redcarpetcrash.com

Their Phoenix category posts passes roughly a day before the aggregators. Worth checking direct. Mind on Movies' own front page is still serving 2018 archive content, so treat it as unreliable.

Harkins email & Facebook

Where both Dune drops were announced first, with no press release and about thirty minutes' notice. The single highest-value thing on this list to subscribe to.

harkins.com/events-and-series

Anime at Harkins, Studio Ghibli, Tuesday Night Classics, Movies In 35MM, Kubrick Spotlight. New bookings appear under a series before they appear anywhere else. The store's Tickets & Vouchers page is where series passes surface.

Pollack Tempe Cinemas

Currently polling on Facebook for future classic series. LOTR, the Mummy trilogy, Twilight and Stephen King are on the ballot. A vote is free.

Six IMAX screens exist in the metro: Harkins Arizona Mills (the only one in Arizona with 70mm film capability), AMC Mesa Grand, Desert Ridge, Deer Valley, Westgate, and Superstition East.

Everything above was verified on the date shown. Sold-out status, showtimes and pass availability change fast — confirm before you drive.
